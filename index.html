FAQ

Still got questions?
We've got answers.

Is Sptix a full Applicant Tracking System?

Yes. Sptix is a complete ATS: job posting with an AI-written JD, a public jobs page for your company, an applicant pipeline with custom stages, candidate profiles with notes and tags, structured interview scorecards, team roles and permissions, and hiring analytics. AI screening and ranking sit on top of that core instead of replacing it.

How is Sptix different from Greenhouse or Lever?

Those are strong US-first ATS products. Sptix is built for Indian hiring: Indian salary benchmarks, an Indian skill graph, WhatsApp-first candidate comms, and AI screening included rather than sold per seat — at a price Indian teams can actually sign off.

How does the AI hiring software work?

AI parses every resume, scores applicants on 40+ fit signals against your JD, flags dealbreakers, and produces a ranked shortlist with written reasoning — before you open the inbox. You stay in control: every score is explainable and every action is yours to approve.

Can I migrate from another ATS?

Yes — bulk import jobs, candidates, and history from CSV or from your existing ATS. Most teams finish migration and run their first Sptix pipeline within a week, with hands-on help from our team during the pilot.

Is there a free trial?

Every company plan starts with a 7-day free trial. Nothing is charged until it ends, you can cancel anytime, and you'll be inside the product in 60 seconds.

What integrations are supported?

Google and Outlook calendars for scheduling, email sync, job boards, WhatsApp notifications, and webhooks/API for everything else. Native integrations ship free — no middleware tax.

Does Sptix support remote and global hiring?

Yes. Remote, hybrid and multi-location roles are first-class — with timezone-aware scheduling, region-based salary benchmarks, and pipelines for contractors and global entities.

Is there a vendor / agency portal?

Yes — recruiting partners get a tracked portal to submit candidates straight into your funnel, with duplicate detection, source attribution and per-vendor performance analytics.

How does pricing work?

One product, one price — per company, sized by plan, not per seat. AI screening, analytics and every module included. No per-feature add-ons, no surprise line items at renewal.

How long does implementation take?

Minutes, not months. Sign up, create your company, and post your first AI-drafted role the same day. Data migration from an existing ATS typically completes inside a week.
